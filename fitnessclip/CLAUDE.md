# @fitnessclip: fitness and gym clip account

This file replaces the root `CLAUDE.md` whenever a command is run for @fitnessclip. Ignore the @sidelinesync and @kickIIP rules
when working on this account.

Goal: 10 posts a day of the most viral fitness and gym clips, with credit to the creator.
We don't need to be first. We need clips that make people stop scrolling, reply, save and repost.

## Tools
Same as the other accounts: Playwright (the same research account, read only: never like, follow, post, reply or DM),
vidIQ (TikTok/Instagram/YouTube outliers), and yt-dlp for downloads. Never log into TikTok, Instagram or YouTube.

## Files (all inside `fitnessclip/`)
- `fitnessclip/watchlist.md`: creators, repost accounts and X search queries.
- `fitnessclip/posted.md`: every clip posted on @fitnessclip. Never repost a clip.
- `fitnessclip/slates/YYYY-MM-DD.md`: each day's slate.
- `fitnessclip/backlog.md` and `fitnessclip/clips/backlog/`: proven classics to fall back on (60 days between reposts).
- `fitnessclip/clips/<date>/`: downloaded videos (on the PC only).

## Niche check
A clip is in the niche if it's one of these:
- **Strength and skill**: big lifts, calisthenics and gymnastics skills, strongman, powerlifting, grip and balance feats.
- **Gym moments**: funny or wholesome gym clips, spotter saves, gym-buddy moments, creators' own fails and skits.
- **Inspiring**: older lifters, adaptive athletes, comebacks, first pull-up or first big PR, training partners.
- **Athletes training**: pro athletes' workouts and conditioning drills.
- **Useful**: short, clearly correct form tips from qualified or well-known coaches.

Reject it, whatever its numbers, if it's:
- body shaming, or mocking someone's body, weight or appearance
- secretly filmed strangers at the gym, "gym creep" call-outs, or a pile-on against a private person
- injuries, bad form that ends in a real injury, or dangerous challenges people might copy
- steroid, SARMs or supplement promotion, sponsor reads, ads, giveaways, coupon codes
- diet or medical claims (fat-loss hacks, "this cures…"), extreme cutting or dehydration content
- sexualized clips where the "fitness" is just a pretext
- politics, hate, AI-generated clips, or staged clips passed off as real (obvious comedy skits are fine)

Look at the video itself before suggesting it. If unsure, leave it out.

## Credit
- Credit the person who filmed or posted it first: `🎥: @creator`. Repost accounts are where we find clips, not who we credit.
- If the original can't be found, use `via @account` and rank it lower. Keep the original watermark on the video.

## Rules for picking clips
- Prefer clips from the last 24–72 hours with high engagement for their account size; fitness clips age slower than sports.
- 5–60 seconds, understandable with the sound off, the payoff in the first 3 seconds.
- Mix: about 4 strength/skill, 3 gym moments (funny or wholesome), 2 inspiring, 1 athlete training or tip.
  No more than 2 from one creator per day.
- Skip anything already all over the timeline for 2+ days.
- Game or practice highlights (a dunk, a lob, a goal) are @sidelinesync clips, not fitness. Athlete clips only count here
  when the training itself is the point (conditioning, lifting, drills), and at most 1 a day.
- Prefer clips with a findable creator. Fitness creators post their own clips, so a "via @repost account" credit should be rare.

## Caption style
- Hook first, under ~100 characters, conversational. Emojis on most captions (1–2): 😳🤯 wild, 💪🔥 strong, 😭😂 funny, 🥹 wholesome, 💀 fail.
- No hashtags (or 1 at most), no links. Credit on its own line.
- Only state numbers you can confirm (weights, reps, age). If unsure, leave them out. No health or diet advice in captions.
- Every few posts, end with a question that gets replies ("Could you do one rep?", "What's your max?", "Rate the form 1–10").

## Backlog classics (for `/backlog fitnessclip`)
Iconic gym and strength clips that get reposted again and again: famous lifts, legendary calisthenics skills, strongman
moments, the funniest gym fails (no injuries), and wholesome older-lifter clips. Search X with no date limit and high
thresholds (e.g. `(gym OR lift OR deadlift OR "pull ups" OR calisthenics) filter:native_video min_faves:30000 <exclusions>`).
Captions for a classic: "This will never get old 😭", "Still one of the strongest clips ever 🤯".
