# @kickIIP backlog: proven viral clips to fall back on

Clips that have already gone viral (often many times) and still work as reposts. Use them when a slate is short,
when a planned clip turns out to be stale, or for one "classic" slot a day.
Videos are in `kickiip/clips/backlog/` (on the PC only). Fill this with `/backlog kickiip 20`.

Rules:
- Don't repost a backlog clip within 60 days of its last use. When one is used, fill in "Used on" and add it to `posted.md`.
- Count backlog clips toward each day's mix and per-sport limits like any other clip.
- Keep it to about 40–60 unused clips; refill with `/backlog` when it drops below 20.
